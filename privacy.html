Privacy policy

RiderMe helps a group plan a trip together and split what it costs. This page says exactly what it holds, who else sees it, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 22 September 2026

Who is responsible

RiderMe is an independent app, reachable at help@riderme.app. Write to that address about anything on this page, including any of the requests below.

RiderMe does not ask for and does not store: your phone number, your address, any payment card or bank details, your contacts, your photos, or your device's location. Cost splitting is bookkeeping only — no money moves through RiderMe, so there is nothing to take payment details for.

What is kept on your own device

Some things are stored in your browser rather than on a server, so the app works offline and opens quickly:

  • A cached copy of the riders you have open.
  • Changes you make while offline, waiting to be sent when you reconnect.
  • Small markers: which discussions you have read, which reminders you have dismissed, and coordinates already looked up for locations, so the same place is not looked up twice.

All of it is cleared when you sign out. It never leaves your device except as the changes themselves being saved.

Who else sees it

RiderMe does not sell your data, and does not share it for advertising. These are every outside service the app connects to, and exactly what each one receives:

ServiceWhat it receives
SupabaseHosts the database, the accounts and the sign-in and invitation emails. Everything in the table above is stored there. Supabase acts on our instructions and does not use it for anything of their own.
Google AnalyticsThat a page was opened, with a randomly generated identifier for your browser, plus the general information any web request carries such as your IP address, browser and rough region. It is deliberately told the address of the page WITHOUT any query string, so invite codes and sign-in links are never sent to it.
OpenStreetMapTwo things. Map images are fetched from their tile servers, which shows them your IP address and which part of the map you are looking at. And when you type a location into an item, that text is sent to their search service to turn it into coordinates.
frankfurter.devCurrency codes only, when a rider uses more than one currency, so an exchange rate can be fetched. No personal information is sent.
Push servicesIf you turn notifications on, your browser registers with its own push service — Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your device — and notifications reach you through it.

RiderMe may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Other people on your riders

A rider is shared, so the people in it can see your display name, what you add and change, your comments, your votes and your share of any cost. That is the point of the app rather than a side effect, and it is worth being clear about: anything you put on a shared rider is visible to everyone else on it. Your private list is the exception and is visible to nobody.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and its data are kept while the account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app or by asking us — how to delete your account sets out exactly what is removed and the little that is kept.
  • Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which it is gone.
  • Rate-limit records are short-lived and are removed with your account.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it in a particular way. Email help@riderme.app and we will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or the EU

We rely on these lawful bases: performing our agreement with you, for running your account and your riders; legitimate interests, for keeping the service secure and working, including the rate limits; and consent, for push notifications and for analytics, which you can withdraw at any time. You also have the right to object, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

If you are in California

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we do not discriminate against anyone who exercises their rights.

Where your data is held

RiderMe's database and accounts are hosted by Supabase, and the services listed above operate internationally, so your information may be processed outside your own country — including in the United States. Where that involves a transfer out of the UK or the EEA, it is made under the safeguards those providers have in place, such as the standard contractual clauses.

Children

RiderMe is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email help@riderme.app and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to a rider is enforced by the database itself rather than only by the app, so a rider you are not a member of cannot be read even by a request made outside the app. Passwords are stored as hashes, never in a readable form. No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change is significant we will say so in the app rather than relying on you to re-read this page.
