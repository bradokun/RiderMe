Delete your RiderMe account

RiderMe is at riderme.app. You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app or by asking us — you do not need to install anything.

If you still have the app

  1. Open RiderMe and sign in.
  2. Open the menu, then Security.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and enter your password to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If any of your data cannot be removed, the account is not deleted and you are told why — an account is never closed while data belonging to it is still on the server, because there would then be nobody left who could ask for it.

If you no longer have the app

Email help@riderme.app from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account.

  • We reply within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • We will ask you to confirm from that same address. We cannot delete an account on a request we cannot tie to it — otherwise anyone could close anyone else's.

● What is removed completely

  • Your login — your email address and password.
  • Your profile: your display name and your list of riders.
  • Your private list — every item you kept to yourself, in every rider.
  • Your membership of every rider, so you lose access to all of them.
  • Your votes and your “are you coming?” answers, so you stop being counted on anyone's list.
  • Your notifications, and your read markers in discussions.
  • The internal rate-limit records that track how often you invited or joined.
  • Your push notification registrations — every device RiderMe could have sent a notification to.
  • Any rider you were the last full-access member of is deleted outright: its items, discussions, history, costs, invite codes, and every member's private list for it.

● What stays, and why

A rider that other people are still using is their rider too. Removing your contributions from it would edit their copy of a shared plan, so these stay:

  • Items you added, and the change history of the rider, which may still show the name you used.
  • Comments you wrote in a rider's discussions.
  • Cost and settlement records naming you. These are what other members' balances are calculated from — deleting a settlement would silently re-open a debt that had been settled.

None of this is linked to a login any more, and none of it can be used to sign in. If you want your name removed from a rider you have left, email help@riderme.app and we will handle it with the people still in it.

Backups

Deleted data can persist in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days, after which it is gone. Backups are never used to restore a deleted account.
